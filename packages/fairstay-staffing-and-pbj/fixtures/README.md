# Fixtures

One fictional Cedar Hollow week, 23 to 29 March 2026, with 40 residents, checked on 20 April. The example targets are 3.48 total, 0.55 RN and 2.45 aide hours per resident day. Planted:

- Weekdays meet every target.
- Saturday 28 March has RN shifts of 7 to 11, 12 to 16 and 17 to 23, so there is never 8 unbroken RN hours. That day is short on every target.
- Sunday is short on aide hours.
- The agency billed 12 hours on 25 March for a shift our time records show as 8.
- PBJ for the quarter ending 31 March is due 15 May and is not marked submitted.

Shifts that cross midnight count on the day they start. Employee names and ids are in `phi-canaries.json`; they must never reach the model. `rules-typo.json` misspells a setting, so the check refuses it.
