# Fixtures

A fictional Cedar Hollow 13-week projection starting Monday 6 April 2026. Planted:

- A pharmacy bill due 2 April is already past due, so it lands in week 1.
- Week 1 has payroll, insurance and that bill together, so cash dips 8510 below the 250000 minimum.
- Hospice receivables have no collection rate in the plan, so they are left out and listed.
- The provider tax in week 6 and rent on the 1st of each month are spread through the weeks.

Bank account numbers are listed in `phi-canaries.json`; they must never reach the model. `plan-typo.json` misspells a setting, so the projection refuses it.
